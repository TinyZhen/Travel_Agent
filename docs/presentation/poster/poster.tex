  \blockhead{Who it is for, and who else helps plan trips}
  Built for students on short trips, budget travelers and small groups who split the cost, first student clubs. A club of four sees 2 rooms and the cost per person before anyone pays. The code is public on GitHub.\par\vspace{0.08in}
  Chat assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini answer in a chat. Expedia's Romie helps plan, shop and book inside Expedia [3]. Planner apps such as Mindtrip, Layla and Wonderplan build itineraries, says a review [4]. Others use spreadsheets, group chats or a travel agency. We have not tested the others.\par
