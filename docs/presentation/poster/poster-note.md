# Roameo: two-minute poster pitch

Speakers: Zhen Lu and Noctis Zhang. Pace: about 140 spoken words per minute. Budget: 120 seconds
at most, planned 113. Numbers are written as they are spoken. Point at the poster as you go: left column,
then the centre, then the right. Alex is an illustration: say so.

## Pitch 1. Alex and the $600 (left column, top)
_about 19 seconds_

Hi, we are Zhen Lu and Noctis Zhang. This is Roameo, an AI trip planner for students and budget
travellers. Meet Alex, a student we made up. Alex has six hundred dollars and types one sentence:
Chicago for four days, budget six hundred dollars.

## Pitch 2. What the agents did (left column)
_about 30 seconds_

This is one real run of the app. Roameo first asked where Alex was leaving from, and when. It
never guesses. Then its agents, AI helpers with one job each, found eight flights, eighteen hotels
and fifty nine events. The first plan cost one thousand two hundred thirty five dollars. The
critic, which is plain code and not the AI, repaired it five times, down to seven hundred eighty.

## Pitch 3. The human decision (centre, top)
_about 20 seconds_

Still one hundred eighty over. So it stopped and asked, right here: raise the budget, shorten to
two nights, or drive? The agents do the work. Alex makes the call, and nothing is final until Alex
approves. Approving only exports a calendar file. Nothing is ever booked.

## Pitch 4. How it works and the evidence (centre, then right)
_about 32 seconds_

Here is how: AI agents gather, plain code checks the money, and you decide twice. On the right:
we wrote twenty five test conversations. All twenty three core tests pass in demo mode, on rules,
and in AI mode, in each of three runs. Most of them check that it asks you, and takes feedback like
cheaper or skip a place. They are our own tests, on sample prices, so it is a small check.

## Pitch 5. Try it (right, bottom)
_about 12 seconds_

It runs without any keys in about three minutes, and deploys as one web address, with any OpenRouter
model. Scan the code to try it. Thank you.
