# Roameo, the two-minute poster pitch

Speakers Zhen Lu and Noctis Zhang. About 140 spoken words a minute, 120 seconds at most. Numbers are written as they are spoken. Point at the poster as you go, the strip at the top, then the three columns from left to right, then the bottom band. Alex is a student we made up, so say so.

> Before judging, team actions that cannot be scripted here.
> 1. Record a 30 to 40 second screen capture of a live run of Alex's sentence, as a backup at the table. It needs the team's own keys.
> 2. Open the free live demo a minute before judging, so it is awake.
> 3. Scan both QR codes with a phone. If an access code is set on the live demo, give it at the stand.

## The short version, Alex's trip (the strip at the top)
_about 36 seconds_

> If a visitor has only half a minute, say this part and stop.

Alex, a student we made up, has six hundred dollars for four days in Chicago and types one sentence. Roameo asks where from and when. Then its AI agents compare flights, hotels and events. Plain code adds up the prices. The first plan costs one thousand two hundred thirty five dollars. Five fixes bring it to seven hundred eighty, still one hundred eighty over. So Roameo stops and asks Alex. Raise the budget, stay two nights, or drive? Nothing is booked until Alex says OK.

## Idea one, AI agents do the legwork (left column)
_about 15 seconds_

Roameo rests on three ideas. First, AI agents do the legwork. An agent here is an AI helper with one job. They ask for what is missing, choose the searches, and turn your words into changes.

## Idea two, code checks the money (middle column)
_about 12 seconds_

Second, code checks the money. Plain code adds up every total, tries one fix at a time, at most five, and shows the gap instead of hiding it.

## Idea three, you make the call (right column)
_about 13 seconds_

Third, you make the call. You choose how to close the gap, and you see every step. Saying OK only downloads a calendar file. Roameo never books or pays.

## Tests, other apps and your data (bottom band)
_about 24 seconds_

A test is a conversation we wrote in advance, with code that checks the answer. All twenty three core tests pass, with and without the AI. It is a small check, not a user study. Chat assistants and booking sites also plan trips, and we have not tested them. Roameo needs no account and labels sample prices.

## Close and the ask (bottom right)
_about 12 seconds_

So, AI agents did the legwork, code checked the money, and Alex made the call. Scan the code to try it free, and tell us where it goes wrong.
